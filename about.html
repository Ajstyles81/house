Our Story

Fourteen years helping people find home

Browse listings

Founded in 2012, HouseAgent began with a simple mission: to transform the real estate experience by putting clients first. What started as a small team of three passionate agents has grown into a trusted agency serving hundreds of families and businesses each year.

We believe that buying, selling, or investing in property should be an exciting journey, not a stressful ordeal. That's why we've built our practice on transparency, expertise, and genuine care for our clients' success.

Our core values guide everything we do: integrity in every transaction, excellence in service delivery, and commitment to building lasting relationships. We don't just close deals — we help people find homes and build futures.
